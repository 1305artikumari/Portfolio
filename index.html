About me

Data with a point of view

Hi, I'm Arti — a Data Analyst at Kantar in Bangalore. I turn messy commercial data into forecasts, models and stakeholder-ready stories for global clients. I am now looking for Data Scientist roles where that same craft goes deeper into production modelling.

My toolkit covers the full data lifecycle: cleaning, wrangling and exploratory analysis with Python, Pandas, NumPy and SQL; interactive Power BI development with DAX; visual storytelling with Tableau; and deep spreadsheet analytics with Excel, pivot tables and Google Sheets. I apply statistical analysis, ETL workflows and BI best practices to deliver accurate, stakeholder-ready reports.

I work with an AI-first approach to analytics — Excel, Power BI, Tableau, SQL and Python together. That includes AI-powered Excel automation, advanced formulas and visualisation, so reporting is faster without losing control of the numbers.

At Kantar the work already sits on the Data Science side: multiple regression, Random Forest, seasonality, lag features and marketing-mix modelling. I validate with R² and error metrics, then hand over Excel dashboards and PPT readouts people can actually use.

Whether it's a Power BI dashboard, a complex SQL query, an automated cleaning script or a KPI report — my goal is to make data feel less like numbers and more like a roadmap for decision-making.

I completed a PG Diploma in Big Data Analytics at CDAC Bangalore (70%) after a B.Tech in Computer Science, with a diploma in CSE before that.
